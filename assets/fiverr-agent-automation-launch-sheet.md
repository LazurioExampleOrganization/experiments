## Description

```text
Do you have a repetitive business process that feels like it should be
automated, but you are not sure where to start?

I will review one workflow and turn it into a practical automation blueprint.
This can include research workflows, lead or account enrichment, reporting,
internal checklists, intake forms, CRM follow-ups, AI-assisted summaries,
routing logic or small operations workflows.

What you get:

- a clear fit / no-fit automation verdict
- the smallest useful automation step
- a workflow map
- recommended tools
- risks, edge cases and approval checkpoints
- a concise handoff document

Tools I can reason about:

- Zapier
- Make
- n8n
- Airtable
- Notion
- Google Sheets
- HubSpot / CRM workflows
- OpenAI or other AI API-assisted steps
- webhooks and APIs

Important:

I do not offer spam automation, fake engagement, scraping that violates terms of
service, credential harvesting, deepfakes, misleading content or automations
that send messages or change external systems without human approval.

If your workflow involves sensitive customer data or access to third-party
accounts, please describe the process first. I will tell you what can be safely
planned and what needs a separate approval or security setup.
```

## FAQ

Question:

```text
Will you build the automation?
```

Answer:

```text
This gig is primarily for workflow triage and blueprinting. If the workflow is
small and safe, I can propose a separate implementation scope after the audit.
```

Question:

```text
Do you guarantee leads, sales or revenue?
```

Answer:

```text
No. I help map and design practical workflows. I do not guarantee business
outcomes that depend on your market, offer, audience or sales process.
```

Question:

```text
Can you work with my private customer data?
```

Answer:

```text
Not in the first step. Start by describing the workflow without sensitive data.
If implementation requires private data, we need a separate security and access
approval process.
```

Question:

```text
Can you automate cold outreach?
```

Answer:

```text
I can help design compliant human-reviewed workflow steps, but I do not build
spam systems, fake engagement systems or automations that bypass platform rules.
```

## Buyer requirements

Required questions:

```text
1. What is the workflow you want to improve?
2. Who performs it today and how often?
3. What tools are involved?
4. What is the desired output?
5. What data or inputs are needed?
6. Is any part sensitive, regulated or tied to customer consent?
7. What should the automation never do?
```

Optional request:

```text
If useful, attach a Loom/video walkthrough, screenshots with sensitive data
removed, sample input/output, or an existing SOP/checklist.
```

## Pre-publish checklist

- Jan confirms Fiverr seller account/onboarding is ready.
- Jan explicitly approves publishing this gig.
- No off-platform payment, external contact, Stripe, Ko-fi, Gumroad or email
  link is included in the gig.
- Pricing is accepted with Fiverr marketplace economics.
- First 3 inbound messages/orders are logged as experiment evidence.
- Any buyer request involving private data, account access or external sending
  gets a separate approval step.

## Run metric

Measure for 14 days after publishing:

- impressions
- clicks
- qualified messages
- orders
- buyer workflow categories
- prohibited/out-of-scope requests
- delivery time

Scale if:

- 1 paid order closes,
- or 3 qualified inbound messages describe a similar workflow category.

Stop or rewrite if:

- messages are mostly spam/scams,
- buyers mostly ask for prohibited automation,
- buyers want full implementation but will not pay for blueprinting,
- delivery time does not fit package economics.
