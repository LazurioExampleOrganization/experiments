# First monetization test checklist

Status: active checklist
Owner: alexmorgan
Last updated: 2026-05-23

Use it before every experiment that aims to validate first revenue or qualified
demand for Lazurio Example Organization.

## 1. Hypothesis

- It clearly states who has the problem.
- It clearly states what they could pay for.
- The hypothesis can be refuted within 7-14 days.
- It is not just "people want AI"; it is a specific workflow or outcome.

## 2. Offer

- The offer has one main outcome.
- The scope is limited so that it can be delivered manually or semi-agentically.
- The price or the paid-pilot question is specific.
- It is clear what is not part of the offer.

## 3. Target customer

- The ICP is described more specifically than "SMB" or "founders".
- There is an accessible channel where a first signal can be gained without a
  paid campaign.
- The company does not need other people's customer data, non-public databases
  or access to third-party accounts to prepare the test.

## 4. Metric

The experiment must have at least one primary metric:

- a paid pilot
- a refundable deposit
- a qualified discovery call with clear pain and willingness to pay
- a written "yes, send me an offer / invoice"

Vanity metrics such as likes, traffic or a positive comment are not enough.

## 5. Budget and time

- The money budget is stated in advance.
- The time budget for founder/agent work is stated in advance.
- The stop date is a specific date.
- No paid tool, ad or SaaS subscription starts without human confirmation.

## 6. Stop / scale decision

These must be filled in before the start:

- Stop condition: what it means that the experiment does not work.
- Scale condition: what it means that the experiment is worth another
  iteration.
- Pivot note: what learning would lead to a change of ICP, offer or price.

## 7. Safety and reputation

The experiment must not include:

- spam or mass unsolicited outreach
- fake engagement, fake accounts or stealth posting
- phishing, credential harvesting or misleading claims
- scraping against ToS or bypassing rate limits
- copying someone else's brand, content, database or customer data

External actions that require explicit human confirmation:

- sending an email, DM or form
- publishing a landing page or public content
- spending money
- using customer data
- signing in to a third-party account
- running an automation that communicates outside the local workspace

## 8. Evidence

Before the start, there is a place to record the result:

- an experiment spec in `specs/`
- a run log in `runs/`
- links to anonymized evidence
- a decision: stop, repeat, scale or pivot
